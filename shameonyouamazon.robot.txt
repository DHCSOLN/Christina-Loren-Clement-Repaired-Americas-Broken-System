Christina Clement Calls for Immediate Investigation Into Broken Amazon Links and Substantially Reduced Book Prices

Author raises concerns over access to her published work and the impact on her revenue

ATLANTA, GA — Author Rev. Dr. Christina Clement is calling for an immediate investigation into problems affecting her published work on Amazon. Clement reports that every Amazon publication link she has checked leads to a broken or unavailable page and that her books are being offered at prices substantially below those she initially established.

Clement views these issues as a direct attack on her ability to earn revenue from her intellectual property. Whether the problems resulted from technical errors, marketplace practices, or intentional interference has not been established.

“When readers cannot access my books through the links provided, my ability to reach customers and generate sales is harmed,” Clement said. “When prices appear substantially below what I originally set, I deserve a clear explanation of who changed them, why, and how my royalties are affected. My work represents years of research, writing, and investment.”

Clement is requesting that Amazon and any relevant publishing or distribution partners:

* Investigate and repair affected publication links.
* Review each title’s availability, seller, edition, and displayed price.
* Explain discrepancies between her established prices and current offers, including any retailer discounts or third-party listings.
* Provide an accounting of applicable royalty calculations and any account-level pricing changes.
* Preserve relevant listing, pricing, sales, and account records.

A displayed retail discount alone does not establish that an author’s royalty has been reduced. Clement is seeking records to determine the actual financial impact and identify the source of the discrepancies.

“Authors deserve accessible listings, transparent pricing, and accurate compensation,” Clement said. “I am asking for prompt corrective action and a documented explanation.”

Readers who encounter an unavailable listing or a pricing discrepancy are encouraged to retain the URL, screenshot, date, book title, and seller information to support the review.
