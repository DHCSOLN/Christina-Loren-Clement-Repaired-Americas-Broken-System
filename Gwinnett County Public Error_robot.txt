Christina Clement Calls on Gwinnett County to Correct Confusing Presentation of Her Name on Write-In Candidate Notice

ATLANTA, GEORGIA — Christina Clement is calling on Gwinnett County Voter Registrations and Elections to immediately correct the formatting of its “Final List of Certified Write-In Candidates” for the November 3, 2026 election.

In the notice shown, marked “Revised 9.29.26,” the U.S. Senate candidates’ names run together across multiple lines. “Christina” appears immediately after “Bartell,” while “Clement” appears on the following line immediately before “Cassandra Easton.” This presentation splits Christina Clement’s name and fails to clearly distinguish the individual candidates.

“My name is Christina Clement. Voters should be able to read my full name clearly, on its own line, under the office I am seeking,” Clement said. “An official election notice should help voters identify candidates accurately. This formatting creates unnecessary confusion and needs immediate correction.”

Clement is requesting that the county publish a corrected notice displaying each U.S. Senate write-in candidate separately:

Elbert Arthur Bartell
Christina Clement
Cassandra Easton

She is also requesting a review of online and printed versions of the notice, replacement of affected copies, and written confirmation explaining how the correction will be communicated to election workers and voters.

“This is about accurate identification and equal visibility in official election information,” Clement said. “I am asking Gwinnett County to correct the notice promptly and make the corrected version readily available to the public.”
