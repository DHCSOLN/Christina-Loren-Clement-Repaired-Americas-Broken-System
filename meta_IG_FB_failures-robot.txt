CHRISTINA CLEMENT ISSUES PUBLIC NOTICE OF INTENT TO INSTAGRAM, FACEBOOK, AND META

Creator demands human support and accountability when account-access problems threaten livelihoods

ATLANTA, GEORGIA — Christina Clement is issuing this public Notice of Intent directly to Instagram, Facebook, and their parent company, Meta, calling for accessible human support, transparent account-access procedures, and accountability when unresolved platform problems affect creators’ ability to earn income.

“Silent attacks are real—the ones people are too busy to recognize as a problem,” Clement said. “But when you earn revenue from these platforms and suddenly cannot log in on the first of the month, with no one to call and only automated bots responding, what happens next? Your bills are still due. Everyone around you has their own struggles. Where do you turn?”

Clement describes these concerns as a matter of economic security for people who depend on digital platforms to reach customers, promote their work, and support their families. She is calling for an investigation into her reported access concerns and any resulting disruption to her business. Intentional interference and the extent of any revenue loss have not been independently established.

Clement is requesting that Instagram, Facebook, and Meta:

* Provide a human representative to review her reported account-access concerns.
* Explain any restrictions, authentication failures, or account actions identified during that review.
* Restore access where an error is confirmed and provide a clear resolution process.
* Preserve relevant account-access, support, monetization, and enforcement records.
* Provide a written response addressing her concerns and the corrective actions taken.

Addressing the companies directly, Clement stated:

“@Instagram @Facebook @Meta: You cannot stop GOD’s plan. Cheaters never win. This is my Notice of Intent.”

Clement says she intends to document the problems, pursue answers, and seek accountability for any verified interference with her access or earnings.

“To the 91 million people I am calling on: make your voices heard,” Clement added. “Georgia voters: Write in Christina Clement for U.S. Congress. ASAP.”
