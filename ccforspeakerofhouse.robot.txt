Christina Loren Clement Declares Readiness to Assume the Role of Speaker of the U.S. House
ATLANTA, GEORGIA — Christina Loren Clement announced that she is prepared to assume the role of Speaker of the U.S. House of Representatives upon election by the House. She is calling on members to put her name in nomination and support a day-one agenda for constituent service, legislative accountability, and restitution.
“I am prepared to take the gavel and get to work,” Clement said. “I will ask every member to come to the table ready to make decisions and stand behind their votes. The American people deserve a House that hears their concerns, debates the issues openly, and puts its decisions on the record.”
Clement, founder and president of State of Loc Nation Global Public Benefit Corporation, enters the race with work already underway. Her campaign built a system that lets people submit concerns in their own words when a congressional contact form has no category for their issue. She has also published a proposed amendment to H.R. 40 that lays out a restitution framework for historically redlined and underfunded communities.
Clement’s first priorities as Speaker
Make every constituent concern count. Work with House offices to make it easier for people to submit concerns, receive a response, and find out what action their office took.
Put restitution before the House. Ask the relevant committees to take up her proposed amendment to H.R. 40 and work with members toward a public hearing, floor debate, and a recorded vote.
Make the House accountable for its decisions. Publish a clear legislative schedule so the public can see what is coming up for debate, what is awaiting action, and how members vote.
“My ask to the members of the House is direct. Put my name in nomination and cast your vote for Christina Loren Clement. I am ready to convene the House, set the legislative agenda, and begin the work on day one.”
