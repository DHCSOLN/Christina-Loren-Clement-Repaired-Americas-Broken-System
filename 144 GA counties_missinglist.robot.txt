CHRISTINA CLEMENT DEMANDS WRITE-IN CANDIDATE TRANSPARENCY ACROSS ALL 159 GEORGIA COUNTIES

Preliminary website review confirms 14 county postings; Clement demands verification of voter access statewide.

ATLANTA, GEORGIA — Christina Clement, a certified write-in candidate for United States Senate, demands immediate confirmation that Georgia’s certified write-in candidate list is available at every early-voting and Election Day polling location across all 159 counties.

A preliminary review confirmed that 14 county websites have posted or linked the certified list. Another 144 counties’ online postings remain unverified, while Forsyth County’s posting appeared in search results but its PDF verification remains pending. These findings concern online access and do not establish whether individual polling places have the required list.

The Georgia Secretary of State’s official certified list identifies Christina Clement as a qualified write-in candidate for U.S. Senate in the November 3, 2026, General Election.

“Certification must be accompanied by meaningful access to information,” Clement stated. “Voters deserve to know every certified choice. I demand public confirmation that the correct list is available at every voting location throughout Georgia.”

THE EFFECT ON VOTERS

When certified write-in information is difficult to find, voters may remain unaware of qualified candidates, arrive without knowing the certified names, or mistakenly believe their choices are limited to the names printed on the ballot. These information gaps can create confusion, hinder informed participation, and disadvantage write-in candidates.

The preliminary review does not establish lost votes or deliberate suppression. It identifies an unresolved transparency concern that election officials can address through prompt verification and clear public information.

DEMAND FOR IMMEDIATE ACTION

Under O.C.G.A. § 21-2-401(d), election superintendents must provide copies of the certified write-in candidate list at polling places. Prominent website publication would provide additional access before voters arrive.

Clement calls upon the Georgia Secretary of State Elections Division and all county election superintendents to:

* Confirm that all 159 counties received the correct certified list.
* Verify its availability at every early-voting and Election Day polling location.
* Correct any documented omissions or inaccurate candidate names immediately.
* Publish the certified list prominently on county election websites.
* Provide a public status report within 24 hours identifying completed checks and corrective action.

Clement also calls upon the Georgia State Election Board to investigate documented failures to provide the required list.

“Voters should not have to investigate whether their county is providing complete election information,” Clement stated. “Make the list accessible. Verify it at the polls. Address every documented omission immediately.”
